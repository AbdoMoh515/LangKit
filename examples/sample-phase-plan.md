# Phase Plan — phase-01: Foundations

> Written by the agent during planning. This file is the human-readable
> phase plan; machine state lives in learner/state.json.

## Objective

Learner can greet, thank, apologize, and ask simple questions in Indonesian,
recognizing and producing ~120 high-frequency travel words with correct
pronunciation habits.

## Target competencies

| Competency | Required | Critical |
|---|---|---|
| greetings-courtesy | yes | yes |
| numbers-prices | yes | yes |
| listening-basic | yes | yes |
| reading-basic | yes | no |
| vocab-travel-core | yes | no |

## Relevant skills

vocabulary, listening, speaking (per learner profile)

## Learning topics

- pronouns (saya, kamu, Anda) and basic sentence frame
- sapaan and time-specific greetings (selamat pagi/siang/sore/malam)
- courtesy: terima kasih, maaf, permisi, tolong
- numbers 1–100, prices, "berapa?"
- question words: apa, di mana, berapa, siapa
- food/drink basics: makan, minum, pesan

## Estimated study effort and timeline

~40 sessions × 30 min ≈ 5–6 weeks at the learner's fixed daily budget.
Estimate, not contract.

## Progression criteria

- core vocabulary recalls reliably across spaced days (registry evidence)
- simple sentences produced without scaffolding
- listening: understands slow, clear single-sentence audio

## Phase test definition

Mixed assessment covering every required competency above: recognition,
active recall (both directions), listening items, short guided conversation.
Blueprint and scoring live in `test.md`. Pass policy: overall ≥ 80%, every
required competency assessed, no critical competency failed, at most one
required non-critical competency below threshold.
