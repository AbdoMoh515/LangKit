# Learning Plan — Indonesian for Travel

> Generated during `/lang-start`. Estimates are estimates, not contracts.
> The plan is adaptive; significant changes are explained to the learner and
> history is preserved in git.

## Learner summary

- Target: Indonesian (beginner) · Source: English (native)
- 30 min/day, fixed · goal: 6 months → basic travel conversation
- Focus skills: vocabulary, listening, speaking

## Phases

| Phase | Title | Milestone | Est. effort | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| phase-01 | Foundations | survive basic greetings + courtesy | ~40 sessions | phase test ≥80%, all required competencies |
| phase-02 | Everyday Communication | handle shops, food, directions | ~50 sessions | phase test ≥80%, all required competencies |
| phase-03 | Travel Basics | transport, lodging, emergencies | ~45 sessions | phase test ≥80%, all required competencies |

Dependencies: each phase builds on the previous; phases merge to `main` only
after their test passes.

## Phase 1 — Foundations (detail)

- **Objective:** greet, thank, apologize, ask simple questions; recognize and
  produce ~120 high-frequency travel words.
- **Target competencies:** greetings/courtesy (critical), numbers+prices
  (critical), listening-basic (critical), reading-basic, vocab-travel.
- **Topics:** pronouns, sapaan, terima kasih/minta maaf, numbers, question
  words, food basics.
- **Estimated effort:** ~40 sessions × 30 min.
- **Progression criteria:** stable recall of core vocabulary across spaced
  days; can form simple sentences without support.
- **Phase test:** mixed recognition + active recall + listening + short
  guided conversation; blueprint in `phases/phase-01/test.md`.
