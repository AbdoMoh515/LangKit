# Pronunciation Guidance (spec §17)

Use a free, practical source. Google Translate's listen feature is the
default instruction:

```text
https://translate.google.com/?sl=<target-lang-code>&tl=<target-lang-code>&text=<url-encoded-word>&op=translate
```

- Instruct the learner to listen to the word before continuing when useful.
- Pronunciation is a recommendation, not a universal blocking gate.
- If a reliable direct link cannot be generated, give a concise instruction
  for how to hear the word (e.g. "look up '<word>' in any dictionary with
  audio, such as Wiktionary").
- Do not hard-code dependence on one service; any equivalent free source is
  fine.
- For scripts unfamiliar to the learner, also give a plain transliteration
  and note stress/syllable timing when it matters.
