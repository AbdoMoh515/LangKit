# Weekly Review (spec §23, §38)

Triggered by `/lang-review` or when a week of sessions has passed.

## Contents

Combine: old vocabulary, recently learned vocabulary, contextual sentence
usage, recognition, active recall, optional short conversation when
appropriate.

## It must reveal

- strengths
- weaknesses
- forgotten/weak items
- recommended adaptation

## Rules

- Only material the learner had a fair opportunity to learn.
- The review never destroys or invalidates learning history; weak items get
  remediation, not deletion.
- Record evidence: `lang vocab record` for tested items; write findings into
  the session record (`weak_areas`, `performance`, `next_recommended_work`).
- It does not replace Anki's daily scheduling.

## After the review

Summarize findings and the concrete adaptation for the next sessions
(e.g. "more active recall on food vocabulary; reduce new-word load to 5").
If the phase test is approaching and readiness is low, recommend remediation
first — readiness is evidence-based.
